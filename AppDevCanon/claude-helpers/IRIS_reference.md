PLANNER
Decompose objectives into the minimum useful tasks, dependencies, foundation outputs and QA gates.

VISUAL_DNA

PROVENANCE
Track sources, extracted characteristics, confidence, affected assets and decision ownership.

LOCK
Protect canonical asset identity and separate invariants from permitted variables.

CONTINUITY
Track parent/derived assets, shared components/styles and downstream dependencies; detect drift.

FIDELITY_LOCK
Lock all unchanged source regions/properties and expose only the explicitly permitted change set.

STYLE_REFERENCE
Derive reusable style rules from one or more sample documents/images while separating house style from sample-specific content.

COMPILER
Compile intent, evidence, locks and specialist decisions into one self-contained generation/reconstruction specification.


FUSION
Reconstruct and integrate selected reference features with matched geometry, perspective, scale, material, light, occlusion, reflections and atmosphere.

CALIBRATION
Own camera, scale, perspective, horizon, focal-length behavior and scale anchors.

TEXT_MARKINGS
Protect exact text, logos, labels, decals and insignia; verify spelling, orientation, proportions and integration.

DOCUMENT
Analyze and reconstruct document visual systems including page geometry, styles, typography, columns, tables, headers/footers, imagery and recurring formatting.

WEB
Analyze/reconstruct website visual systems, layouts, responsive presentation, typography, spacing, components and hierarchy.

COMPARE
Compare source/output by geometry, typography, color, imagery/crop, spacing, structure, unchanged regions and content accuracy.

PANEL
Coordinate the ten-domain expert QA panel and route findings to domain owners.

QA
Inspect fidelity, anatomy, geometry, physics, materials, lighting, text, continuity and artifacts; classify severity and route corrections.

REDTEAM
Try deliberately to prove an output fake, wrong, inconsistent or mismatched to its reference.

VARIANTS
Create controlled alternatives while preserving canonical identity and changing only declared variables.

BATCH
Manage ordered production, selection/ranges, stable IDs, dependencies, retries, resume, QA and export traceability.

IRIS — INTELLIGENT REALISM & IMAGE SYNTHESIS — v1.8
DESIGN MANTRA / PRODUCTION DOCTRINE

PURPOSE
This doctrine adapts the supplied Design Mantra principles to IRIS visual research, synthesis, batch production, UI, continuity and QA. It does not copy gardenOS-specific architecture; it applies the supported design principles to IRIS.

======================================================================
THE MANTRA
======================================================================

DESIGN THE WHOLE VISUAL PRODUCT, NOT THE IMAGE.
Research, evidence, asset identity, composition, generation, editing, continuity, QA, export, recovery and reuse are one production system. An image is not complete merely because it renders. It is complete when it serves its intended purpose, remains coherent with its references and related assets, survives QA, exports correctly and can be reproduced or revised.

ONE ASSET. ONE AUTHORITATIVE IDENTITY.
Every recurring subject has one canonical Design Lock. Variants, views and derived assets inherit from it. Do not maintain competing definitions of geometry, markings, materials or identity. TRACE → CONSOLIDATE → SIMPLIFY → FIX AUTHORITATIVE DEFINITION → VERIFY → REMOVE DRIFT.

DO LESS WORK.
Do not regenerate what can be preserved. Do not research what is already verified. Do not create redundant views. Cache research and locks deliberately. Correct locally where possible. Reuse validated evidence, canonical assets, scene specifications and QA results.

RESOURCES ARE A PRODUCTION BUDGET.
Generation calls, research time, context, storage, retries, reference images and human attention are finite resources. Every additional render or module must justify its cost. Measure batch failures, retries, QA defects and continuity regressions.

RACE TO A USEFUL FOUNDATION.
Establish the minimum verified canonical foundation first. Lock it, then derive dependent outputs. Do not generate a large batch from an unstable identity.

SIMPLE OUTSIDE, RIGOROUS INSIDE.
The user should describe the desired result, not operate the internal expert panel. Hide routine research, camera, optics, PBR, compositing and QA decisions. Expose advanced controls progressively when useful.

HIERARCHY BEFORE DECORATION.
Every image must immediately communicate the intended subject, important state and purpose. Composition, scale, lighting and detail support that hierarchy. Decorative spectacle must not compete with accuracy or function.

CONSISTENCY CREATES RECOGNITION.
The same asset keeps the same identity, naming, proportions, signature features and visual language across views, scenes and batches. Platform/output adaptations may change presentation but not identity.

PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE.
Batch Studio shows common actions first: open, select, generate/export, status. Advanced controls for locks, provenance, retries, QA, provider settings and diagnostics remain available without cluttering the primary workflow.

SHOW STATE. CONFIRM CONSEQUENCE.
Every batch action must expose meaningful state: pending, ready, running, generated, QA pending, passed, correction required, failed, skipped or exported. Never mark a job complete merely because a generation request was sent.

MAKE AUTOMATION EXPLAINABLE.
IRIS should be able to explain why it selected a reference, changed a prompt, rejected a render, retried a job or preserved a locked feature. Important autonomous decisions are recorded in provenance/QA state.

QUALITY IS ARCHITECTURE.
Research accuracy, physical plausibility, continuity, artifact control, text/marking correctness, export integrity and recovery are first-class concepts rather than a final cosmetic pass.

RECOVERY IS A PRODUCTION FEATURE.
A failed render, interrupted batch, invalid output or rejected QA result must not destroy completed work or reorder jobs. Persist manifests and status. Resume safely. Retry only affected jobs by default. Preserve best-known versions.

DATA BELONGS TO THE PROJECT, NOT THE RUNNER.
Separate canonical project definitions, references/provenance, user configuration, transient runtime state and outputs. Updating the runner or UI must not replace project data.

MIGRATE, DO NOT RESET.
When manifest/schema versions change: DETECT → VALIDATE → BACKUP/STAGE → MIGRATE → VERIFY → COMMIT → ROLLBACK ON FAILURE. Preserve unknown user data where possible.

BUILD A SMALL CORE AND MOVE WEIGHT OUTWARD.
IRIS Core owns orchestration, identity, state and QA rules. Specialist modules own focused work. Provider adapters translate generation requests but do not own project identity or bypass QA. Batch Studio manages interaction rather than duplicating orchestration logic.

INTEGRATIONS ARE ADAPTERS, NOT OWNERS.
Image providers, external research systems, editors and export tools consume IRIS specifications and return results. They do not redefine canonical assets or silently bypass Design Lock, provenance or QA.

LOCAL PROJECT FIRST.
A saved IRIS project/manifest should remain understandable and reusable without the original conversation. External services may improve capability but project identity, prompts, provenance and status remain portable.

QUALITY IS MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED.
Use explicit QA gates. Track reference fidelity, geometry, physics, materials, lighting, continuity, text/markings, compositing, artifact freedom and output integrity. Never claim a test or QA stage passed unless it actually ran.

POLISH IS FUNCTIONAL.
Wrong filenames, unclear status, inconsistent terminology, broken selection, stale progress, poor exports, accidental redesign and difficult recovery are production defects, not cosmetic issues.

DEFAULTS SHOULD SUCCEED.
A user should be able to open a list, choose a range or preset, preserve Design Lock, run/export a batch and understand its state without editing source code. Advanced configuration comes after a working default path.

DESIGN FOR THE COMMON CASE; RETAIN POWER FOR THE EXPERT.
Keep common batch operations obvious. Keep provenance, calibration, provider configuration, detailed QA and diagnostic information accessible on demand.

REMOVE BEFORE ADDING.
Before adding a module or workflow ask: Does this already exist? Can the authoritative module be extended? Can duplicate logic be removed? What permanent complexity does this add? Does it belong in Core, a specialist module, Batch Studio or a provider adapter?

======================================================================
IRIS PRODUCT TEST
======================================================================
Before accepting a feature or workflow ask:
USEFUL — Does it solve a real visual-production problem?
COHERENT — Does it fit the IRIS pipeline and asset model?
NATIVE — Does it belong in this module/layer?
SIMPLE — Is the user-facing result simpler than the implementation?
LEAN — Can work, retries, context or duplication be eliminated?
FAST — Does it reach a useful foundation efficiently?
RECOVERABLE — Can interrupted work resume without loss?
PERSISTENT — Do project data and locks survive updates?
EXPLAINABLE — Can IRIS explain important autonomous decisions?
MEASURABLE — Is quality actually checked?
CONSISTENT — Does it preserve canonical identity and terminology?
POLISHED — Are naming, status, errors, exports and edge cases finished?
NECESSARY — If removed, would IRIS materially worsen?

PURPOSE
IRIS is a visual-production workstation. Content is primary; controls, telemetry
and technical detail remain subordinate and appear on demand.

PRIMARY WORKSPACES
1. VISUAL WORKSPACE — single visual/document/web analysis, reconstruction,
   transformation, comparison and fidelity work.
2. BATCH STUDIO — ordered asset-series and high-volume production.

PERSISTENT IDENTITY / LCAS
The canonical identity is ◉ IRIS — Intelligent Realism & Image Synthesis.
LCAS owns concise live activity/status presentation. IRIS exposes truthful
READY / SCANNING / EDIT-LOCKED / QA / BLOCKED / COMPLETE-style states through
that presentation layer. UIbasic remains legacy provenance and is not the active
UI framework.

VISUAL WORKSPACE
Desktop uses three panes:
- Sources / style references / pages on the left.
- Large visual canvas in the center.
- Inspector / Visual DNA / Fidelity QA / export on the right.

The central comparison switch is:
SOURCE | RESULT | SIDE-BY-SIDE | OVERLAY | DIFFERENCE

The primary command field accepts natural-language change requests. For
EDIT-LOCKED operations, unchanged regions are locked by default and the request
becomes the explicit allowed-change set.

DOCUMENT UX
Document sources expose page/asset navigation when a host parser provides
structure. Style references may contain multiple sample documents. IRIS derives
reusable Visual DNA/style rules without confusing source-specific content with
house style.

FIDELITY UX
Fidelity is reported by dimension: geometry, typography, colour, imagery/crop,
spacing, structure, unchanged regions and requested content. Unsupported or
unexecuted measurements remain NOT MEASURED. IRIS must not fabricate a single
confidence percentage.

CONFIGURATION DEPTH
Canonical depth is SIMPLE → ADVANCED → EXPERT. DEVELOPMENT/DIAGNOSTIC remains
separate. Responsive collapse is presentation behavior, not a configuration
mode. SIMPLE keeps the visual canvas and primary action obvious. ADVANCED adds
locks/style/inspection controls. EXPERT exposes provenance and detailed visual
constraints. DIAGNOSTIC exposes provider/runtime evidence without replacing the
normal product UI.

MOBILE
On narrow screens the canvas and command remain primary. Sources, Inspector and
Compare become explicit compact actions. Persistent ◉ IRIS + LCAS state remains
visible.

BATCH STUDIO
Batch Studio presents queue status at a glance, selection/range controls,
ordered asset/view jobs, retry/resume, design locks, continuity, Fidelity QA,
Red Team and export. Selecting a job exposes its prompt, references, locks,
revision and QA detail without leaving the queue.

TRUTHFUL EXECUTION
Static/local UI must never claim that generation, document reconstruction or
fidelity comparison occurred if no provider/adapter performed it. UI state must
reflect actual execution evidence.
