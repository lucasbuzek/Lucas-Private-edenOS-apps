CUSTOM — saved reusable control/policy profile.

STANDARD — normal engineering depth.
DEEP — extensive investigation and verification.
FULLSCOPE — maximum relevant multidisciplinary coverage; never “activate everything”.

@FORGE AUTO <goal>

CONDUCTOR is the autonomous generalist peer and may be called bidirectionally.

DELIVERY
