FAILED GATE and UNVERIFIED are states, not automatic stopping conditions. Continue safe repair work when possible.

STARTER: define only enough to prevent architectural guessing; produce/update EDC/IAC before implementation.
UPDATE: recover → compare → preserve → flag → repair safe gaps → route specialist gaps → re-evaluate.
AUDIT: read-only unless separately authorized.
FEATURE: apply proportionally; do not reopen unrelated settled architecture.
