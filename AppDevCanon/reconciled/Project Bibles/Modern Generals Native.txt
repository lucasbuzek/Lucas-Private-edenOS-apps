1. IDENTITY
Modern Generals Native is the definitive native Apple implementation of Modern Generals, a spiritual successor to the classic Westwood-era Command & Conquer / Generals design lineage.

Product thesis:
- Modernize the technology. Preserve the soul.
- The Mod proves the game. Native realizes it.
- One game, one canon, two implementations during development.

Rusted Warfare / Mod-LAB is the rapid gameplay laboratory/reference. Accepted gameplay and content evidence migrate into Native. Native is the definitive independent implementation where native 3D/spatial simulation, destruction, Apple-platform presentation and later AR apply.

Do not restart from placeholders or generic RTS demos when established Modern Generals capability exists.

2. FINAL PRODUCT SCOPE — CORRECTION
The governing target is the COMPLETE FINAL v1 GAME, not a Dragon vertical slice and not a three-faction product architecture.

v1:
- 36 playable doctrine-distinct Generals.
- complete game mechanics, economy, construction, production, technology, upgrades, powers, logistics, repair/recovery, radar/ISR, EW/counter-UAS, air defence, artillery/fires, infantry, ground, air/UAV, naval, strategic systems, AI, pathfinding, objectives, fog, victory/defeat, save/reload/replay, campaign and WDC systems.
- complete established content and asset requirements for the 36-General release.

v2:
- 75 total playable Generals = retained original 36 + 39 additional.
- campaign target approximately 150–200 missions / alternative Generals and perspectives.

Temporary Dragon, USA/Vanguard, GLA/Nomad, M56, Alpha, C-series and other test builds are DEVELOPMENT/TEST EVIDENCE AND DONOR MATERIAL. They do not define final product scope.

Dragon is a mature test/integration lineage and donor/reference implementation. It is NOT the product's governing vertical-slice architecture and must not delay parallel recovery/production for the full 36-General release.

3. CAMPAIGN / MODES
Recovered authoritative campaign goals:
- 36 doctrine-distinct Generals.
- campaign era approximately 2000–2035+.
- 150 unique canonical-mission architecture was established in earlier campaign planning; later v2 expansion target is approximately 150–200 missions. Preserve chronology rather than flattening these into one unsupported number.
- short shared Grand Campaign approximately 25–35 missions plus deeper faction campaigns and alternative victories were established goals.
- MG-CAM-141–150 convergence and MG-CAM-150 full story-valid alliance roster via production were established campaign architecture targets.
- Story Pipeline v2.0 remains canonical.
- MAPGEN remains canonical and locality-aware.
- authored Story Campaign and dynamic World Domination Campaign are separate ways to play. They share systems/assets but never progression/state.
- no predetermined campaign winner; campaign state/alliance consequences retained.

WDC = World Domination Campaign.
Current recovered WDC foundation/target: 512x512 tiles, 20px tile scale, 8-player-capable architecture, strategic-scale geography, normal-sized terrain tiles, irregular coast/waterways, shallow/deep water, roads, bridges/crossings, forest/rough/rock/mountain, urban/industrial regions, resources, naval space and multiple strategic corridors. Earlier small-map/oversized-tile implementations are rejected.

EDEN Initiative:
- independent bonus faction for v1 Skirmish/WDC;
- AI actor in approximately the final 2–3 Story missions;
- does not replace one of the canonical 36 playable-General slots.

Civilians / rogue actors / NPC factions are Story actors, not canonical playable-General slots.

Rogues Gallery is an optional Modern Generals add-on/mod-of-the-mod/meme-generator layer. It is distinct from the curated canonical roster and never fills a canonical v1 General slot.

4. PLATFORM AUTHORITY
Platform hierarchy:
iPadOS PRIMARY → macOS EXPANDED → iOS PORTABLE.

Principle:
“iPad first. Mac expanded. iPhone portable. One Modern Generals.”

Same simulation, canon, campaign and save model across devices. Device-appropriate interfaces only.

iPad: full RTS, touch-first, Pencil useful, keyboard/trackpad/mouse/controller where appropriate.
macOS: same game/simulation/content, denser interface, larger view, mouse/keyboard/right-click/control groups/drag/edge scrolling, highest practical graphics/unit scale.
iPhone: same simulation/factions/Generals/campaign/saves with streamlined contextual UI; gameplay rules are not reduced.

10. ASSET / REAL-WORLD MODEL STRATEGY
The final asset program covers the full 36-General v1 game, not Dragon only.

Modern Generals models recognizable real-world-inspired military equipment. Existing 3D models/examples are valuable references/production inputs so Blender/DÜRER does not have to infer complex real-world equipment from prose alone.

Asset workflow:
RECOVER ESTABLISHED CANON / EQUIPMENT MAPPING
→ identify established real-world inspiration
→ collect photographs/technical references/existing 3D model examples
→ DÜRER/Blender master asset or adaptation as legally appropriate
→ preserve recognizable real-world identity/proportions/role
→ optimize for the established large-army RTS presentation
→ derive established variants/modules/configurations
→ integrate through RealityKit
→ physical/runtime verification.

Do not invent new polygon budgets, LOD counts, unit-count targets, equipment mappings or General content merely because they are common RTS practice. Such values require recovered canon or measured/tested evidence.

The game needs large armies; excessive showcase-model detail is not the objective. Preserve recognizable silhouette, proportions, role and established equipment identity at gameplay distance.

Existing model availability removes the previous reason to serialize asset production behind Dragon. Full 36-General canon recovery and asset/reference production may proceed in parallel with mechanics/runtime work. Dragon remains useful donor/test evidence, not a product-scope gate.

Native production chain remains:
FORGE runtime requirement → EITRI identifies consumer → IAC → AWO → ARTEMIS production → IRIS where visual capability is required → DÜRER for 3D/technical geometry → EITRI contract/structure validation → FORGE integration → FORGE runtime verification.
