## NON-DRIFT LOCK
Modern Generals missions/scenarios were already established. Do not replace them with generic RTS mission design.

Primary inspiration/source model:
REAL-WORLD EVENTS / CONFLICTS / CRISES / MILITARY OPERATIONS / GEOPOLITICAL DEVELOPMENTS / TECHNOLOGY
+ EDEN INITIATIVE 25TH-ANNIVERSARY RESEARCH
→ MODERN GENERALS TIMELINE / SCENARIO INTERPRETATION
→ PARTICIPATING FACTIONS / GENERALS
→ DOCTRINE + AVAILABLE TECHNOLOGY
→ MISSION / MAP / OBJECTIVES
→ WORLD STATE + CONSEQUENCES
→ GAMEPLAY ABSTRACTION.

The game uses real-world history and research as inspiration, then adapts it into the fictional/plausible Modern Generals scenario. Game fiction must not be represented as factual history.

The exact source corpus of the EDEN Initiative 25th-anniversary research has not yet been fully recovered. Preserve that as a recovery target; do not invent its missing contents. When recovered, preserve sources, methods, inputs and provenance.

## STORY PIPELINE
Story Pipeline v2.0 is canonical and must be preserved.

Recovered mission-data relationship:
EVENT → MISSION(S) → AVAILABLE GENERAL(S) → OBJECTIVES → WORLD STATE → CONSEQUENCES.

The campaign is driven by a master chronology / causal event ledger rather than disconnected missions. Recovered Story Pipeline goals include causal events, characters, briefs/debriefs, dependencies and alternate outcomes.

Locality matters: missions/maps/assets should fit the event/location rather than forcing inappropriate units or scenery everywhere.

## V1 STORY SCOPE
Current v1 scope is the scaled-down 36-General release. The scale-down did not discard the established campaign architecture.

Locked/recovered v1 rules:
- 36 playable Generals.
- up to 150 unique Story missions is the established v1 ceiling/architecture.
- shared Grand Campaign approximately 25–35 missions.
- faction/General campaigns/perspectives deepen the same world and events.
- choices can affect alliances, interoperability, captured technology and later available force/capability.
- alternative perspectives/outcomes are supported.
- no predetermined geopolitical/campaign winner.
- campaign state and consequences persist according to the authored Story rules.
- scenario content is inspired by real-world events plus EDEN Initiative 25th-anniversary research.

Later expanded 50–60/56-General, 200–300-map and 2000–2040+ planning is historical expanded-scope evidence, not the current v1 scope. Later v2 remains 75 Generals and may expand toward approximately 150–200 missions/perspectives. Do not overwrite v1 with superseded expansion experiments.

## MAPGEN
MAPGEN is canonical.

Recovered rules:
- locality-aware map generation/design;
- reproducible seeds;
- lineage/validation rather than disposable random maps;
- map-specific/local assets where appropriate;
- maps support the mission/event rather than existing independently of campaign context.

Historical development also used an atomic milestone pattern where each General pass included one playable map and cumulative packages preserved previous validated content. This is development-history evidence, not a requirement that the final campaign contain exactly one map per General.

## STORY VS WORLD DOMINATION
Story Campaign and World Domination Campaign (WDC) are separate modes.

Story:
- authored;
- canonical;
- chronology/event/consequence driven.

WDC:
- dynamic;
- non-canonical;
- strategic replay/sandbox campaign.

They may share mechanics, assets, maps and content infrastructure, but NEVER campaign progression/state.

Do not conflate WDC strategic structure with a single tactical map. Recovered 512x512 / 20px / 8-player map work is tactical/operational map evidence, not the complete strategic WDC definition.

Recovered broader WDC planning includes strategic regions/theaters and reusable operational variants. Exact strategic counts/details should be recovered from source evidence rather than invented.

## EDEN INITIATIVE IN STORY
EDEN Initiative is an independent bonus playable faction for v1 Skirmish/WDC and sits outside the canonical 36-General roster allocation.

In Story, EDEN appears AI-controlled only in approximately the final 2–3 missions. Do not introduce it throughout the authored campaign merely because it is playable elsewhere.

Civilians and rogue/non-state Story actors may exist independently without becoming playable General slots.

Recovered EDEN conceptual material includes Architect, Gardener, Shepherd, Voyager, Guardian, Falconer and Tinkerer; cycle SEED → GENERATE → FABRICATE → ADAPT → RECLAIM → EXPAND → RELOCATE. These “Generations” are conceptual/doctrinal evolutions, NOT technology tiers. Their exact historical ordering remains unresolved.

The EDEN 25th-anniversary research is a scenario/research source, not permission to conflate EDEN's playable faction role, its conceptual Generations, or the game's T1–T4 technology system.

## RESEARCH / FICTION BOUNDARY
Real-world events and evidence provide inspiration and grounding. Modern Generals transforms them into game scenarios. Preserve provenance and distinguish:
- historical/real-world source evidence;
- EDEN research/source material;
- Modern Generals fictionalized/plausible scenario interpretation;
- gameplay abstraction.

Do not present fictional game outcomes, alliances, economics or events as factual real-world conclusions.
