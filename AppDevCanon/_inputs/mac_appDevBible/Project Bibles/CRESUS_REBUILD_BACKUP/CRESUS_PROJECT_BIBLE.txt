CRESUS — PROJECT BIBLE
Status: AUTHORITATIVE PROJECT RECONSTRUCTION BASELINE
Date: 2026-10-03

IDENTITY
CRESUS — Capital Research & Equity Strategy. Worldwide investment research, information, organization, portfolio, income and strategy-analysis application family. Inspiration: Croesus, King of Lydia. Design mantra: CRESUS THE KING — sovereign, refined, authoritative, intelligent, premium and enduring; not casino/trading gimmick, crypto neon, generic fintech, developer dashboard or faux-medieval styling. UI/UIX is a primary product requirement developed with functionality.

PURPOSE
CRESUS researches, organizes, calculates, compares and explains. The user decides. Not a broker, automated investment manager or trading bot. Lifecycle: Research -> Evidence -> Thesis -> Portfolio -> ZACCHAEUS -> Strategy Lab -> Intelligence.

WORLDWIDE
No single country, exchange, MIC, currency, tax jurisdiction, market-data provider or AI provider defines Core. Identity: LegalEntity -> Issuer -> Security -> Listing, supporting MIC/exchange, ticker, currency, timezone, trading calendar, ISIN/FIGI/LEI/provider IDs and cross-listings.

TARGETS
Full-feature native applications: iOS/iPadOS, Android, macOS, Windows. Same authoritative CRESUS semantics/deterministic engines with platform-native UI/UX. macOS is current build host. Headless/server/Linux/web/PWA may exist architecturally but do not displace native apps.

PRIVATE APPLE
Use fullest practical Apple-native capability set: Swift/SwiftUI, native persistence, Keychain, App Intents, Shortcuts, Siri/voice, Spotlight, notifications, share sheet/Services, deep links, background APIs, Files/document integration, native networking/charts/accessibility, native device intelligence/Foundation Models or successors, appropriate lifecycle/power capabilities. Full Apple-native AI is a target on supported hardware.

SILENT CHATGPT — PRIVATE APPLE
Private CRESUS may invoke ChatGPT silently behind CRESUS UI when useful. Native/on-device first when adequate. Minimize external context to smallest structured/text representation needed. Do not silently transmit raw screenshots/photos/files/unrelated private data. Portfolio/account/provider data is not a generic AI prompt. Provider output is analysis input, never authoritative financial state. Deterministic CRESUS remains authoritative. Secure secrets appropriately. Provider remains replaceable and non-required for core local functions.

DEVELOPMENT_ALPHA
Everything implemented is unlocked. No CRESUS IAP/subscription/portfolio/watchlist/Expert/Strategy Lab/AI/model/feature limits. Only genuine provider/platform/device constraints apply: provider account/API/rate/model/context/regional limits, connectivity, hardware and OS capability. Commercial enforcement dormant during Alpha.

FIRST OFFICIAL ALPHA AI CONNECTIONS
Implement real provider connection/login architecture: Apple native AI, CRESUS-managed Intelligence gateway, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google/Gemini, Mistral, extensible adapters. Show connected/available/unavailable, capabilities, privacy route, secure credentials, provider limits and usage where exposed. No CRESUS commercial gate in Alpha.

COMMERCIAL MODEL
Obsolete 10%/percentage model is abandoned. Current release model: FREE = bounded discovery/organization; FULL = permanent one-time IAP unlocking complete supported local/on-device product and native/device AI; INTELLIGENCE = recurring IAP/subscription funding CRESUS-managed online research/external evidence/cloud AI; optional user-connected providers under provider terms. Principle: pay once for software, subscribe for continuously costly services. Cancelling Intelligence never removes purchased Full, local data/research/portfolios/watchlists or Full native/device AI.

INTELLIGENCE ECONOMICS
Managed service, not resale of one consumer chatbot subscription per customer. CRESUS users -> pooled Intelligence budget/capacity -> provider-independent gateway -> multiple upstream API/capacity pools. Route by capability, quality, latency, cost, availability, privacy and task class. Prefer adequate native/device AI before marginal cloud spend. Commercial research supports negotiated API capacity/enterprise/volume arrangements, batching/caching and provider diversification.

CORE DOMAIN
LegalEntity, Issuer, Security, Listing, Portfolio, Account, PositionLot, Watchlist, DividendEvent, CorporateAction, PriceObservation, FXObservation, FundamentalObservation, EvidenceRecord, EvidenceConflict, ResearchSnapshot, InvestmentThesis, ThesisRevision, invalidation conditions, StrategyDefinition, BacktestRun, ExpertJob, CalculationLedgerEntry.

TEMPORAL/EVIDENCE TRUTH
Preserve effectiveAt, knownAt, observedAt, sourcePublishedAt, supersededAt, revisionOf and source as applicable. No later information leaking backward. External AI prose is not authoritative evidence. Classes: VERIFIED FACT, CALCULATION, INFERENCE, SCENARIO, UNCERTAINTY, MISSING/UNVERIFIED.

ZACCHAEUS
Dividend & Income Collector; identity The Collector; question: Where's my money? Keep REALIZED, DECLARED, FORWARD INDICATED, ESTIMATED separate. Never silently blend. Lifecycle may include Estimated -> Declared/Announced -> Confirmed -> Ex-Date Passed -> Record Established -> Payable -> Paid -> Reconciled plus changed/deferred/cancelled/missed/unreconciled.

EXPERT COMPLETE-OR-EXHAUST
One invocation continues safe research until COMPLETE or EXHAUSTED/PARTIALLY COMPARABLE after meaningful changed-source/method attempts. Define universe, evidence matrix, detect missing/stale evidence, internal research queue, changed-method retry, post-results event sweep, synchronized comparison-clock integrity, deterministic calculations, evidence challenge, explicit completion gate and Research Snapshot. Do not hand executable known-next research back to user.

RESEARCH SNAPSHOTS
Capture universe, requested time, evidence, prices, FX, macro observations, calculation engine version, provider/model where relevant, evidence matrix, missing/exhausted evidence, completion state, outputs and evidence references.

PORTFOLIO/THESIS
Holdings, quantity, cost basis, listing/currency, current value, realized/unrealized return, income, allocation/exposure, sector/geography/currency exposure, linked research/theses, notes. Thesis: rationale, support/counter-evidence, valuation assumptions, catalysts, risks, invalidation, income expectations, review state, version history.

STRATEGY LAB
SimulationClock: advance -> reveal only evidence known by clock -> evaluate -> simulated trades -> costs/taxes/FX -> corporate actions/dividends -> state. Guard against look-ahead, survivorship, delisting omission, corporate-action errors, revised-fundamental leakage, dividend/adjusted-price double count, stale FX, omitted tax/cost/slippage, benchmark mismatch.

AI ARCHITECTURE
Deterministic CRESUS -> native/device AI -> CRESUS-managed cloud -> optional user-connected provider. Ask for capabilities, not brands. Capabilities include structured.extract, explanation.generate, research.reason, documents.analyse, evidence.challenge, thesis.synthesise, scenario.interpret, deep_examination. Structured outputs where practical; AI produces reasoning/validation proposals, not authoritative state.

UI/UIX
Home/Overview, Research, Watchlists, Portfolio, Calendar/Income/ZACCHAEUS, Strategy Lab, Ask CRESUS, Saved Research/Evidence, Settings/Providers. Simple and Expert are two depths over same truth. No placeholder production screens. Design system: typography, spacing, materials, restrained royal/gilded identity, semantic financial colors, evidence/stale/unverified states, chart conventions, cards/tables/metric rows, loading/empty/error/retry, accessibility.

DATA/PROVIDERS
Private may use user's available sources/APIs/local datasets/browser research/provider credentials without artificial CRESUS licensing restriction. Official is provider-neutral and may use user entitlements/credentials; CRESUS remains responsible for obligations on data it bundles/hosts/redistributes. Track personal-use/user-credential/redistributable/CRESUS-licensed/unknown rights metadata.

OFFLINE/OWNERSHIP
Useful deterministic/local features work offline on stored data. User owns data. Export/recovery: portfolios, holdings, watchlists, theses, notes, saved research, evidence refs, dividends, settings, Strategy Lab definitions/results where practical.
