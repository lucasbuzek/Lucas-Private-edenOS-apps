MONETA ENTERPRISE FINANCE RESEARCH NOTES — v1.1

SOURCE-SUPPORTED FACTS
- COSO Internal Control—Integrated Framework uses five integrated components: control environment, risk assessment,
  control activities, information and communication, and monitoring. It also addresses IT controls and fraud risk.
- IFRS 10 establishes principles for consolidated financial statements where a parent controls subsidiaries and
  presents the group as a single economic entity.
- Current CFO/finance transformation guidance emphasizes tighter coordination across accounting, FP&A, treasury and
  audit, and increased use of analytics/automation/AI with controls and accountability.

EDENOS DESIGN INFERENCES
- MONETA should have a cross-cutting control plane rather than bolting controls onto individual prompts.
- LEDGER needs an explicit Consolidation capability.
- Company boundaries should remain specialist ownership boundaries, while cross-company workflows use formal handoffs.
- AI/provider implementation must remain beneath capability identity.
