Before IMPLEMENT record: requested change; authoritative owner; internal solutions checked; external working
solutions checked; standards/reference implementations; comparison; decision; reason; migration/rollback need;
verification plan; gate status.

FAIL or NOT_RUN blocks IMPLEMENT unless genuinely NOT_APPLICABLE.
BUILD_NEW requires a material gap.
REPLACE keeps the working path authoritative until replacement parity or approved removal is verified.
