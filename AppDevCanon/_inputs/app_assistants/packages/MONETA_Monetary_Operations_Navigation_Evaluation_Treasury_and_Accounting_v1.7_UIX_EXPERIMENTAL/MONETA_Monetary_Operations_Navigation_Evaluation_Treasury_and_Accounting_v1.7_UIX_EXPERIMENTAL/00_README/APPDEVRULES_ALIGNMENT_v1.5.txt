MONETA v1.5 — APPDEVRULES ALIGNMENT
- Existing-Solution-First / implement-integrate before recreate.
- One authoritative capability; multiple surfaces/providers.
- Scale by composition, not by forking.
- Full unified composition on capable hardware; selected specialists/combinations on
  constrained hardware without separate lite forks.
- Local/hybrid/remote provider choice is independent of application composition.
- Recommended / Lean / Full / Custom installation profiles.
- Reuse OS/native capabilities, installed apps, providers and mature components.
- Local-first where practical; user data outlives software.
- Integrations are adapters, not owners.
- Preserve last-known-good state; recovery/rollback are product features.
- Stable IDs survive renames/platform/provider changes.
- Generated ≠ Saved ≠ Tested ≠ Verified ≠ Consolidated ≠ Delivered.
- Specialist domain ownership survives composition into a future larger Business app.
