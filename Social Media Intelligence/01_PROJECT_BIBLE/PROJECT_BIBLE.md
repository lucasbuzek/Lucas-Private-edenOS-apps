# Project Bible — Social Media Intelligence

## Identity
- Scope: Lucas Private / Custom. Tailored to the user's actual accounts, providers, workflows and devices once these are explicitly configured.
- Product role: specialist private governor; independent and self-contained, with its own customized helpers rather than requiring Conductor, Argos or ForgeMASTER at runtime.
- Requested orchestration: `@conductor @argos @forgemaster AUTO EXTENDEDGOAL` is an instruction to prepare and govern the work, not evidence that those systems executed it.
- Primary result: a repeatable pipeline from topic discovery to researched, persona-consistent, platform-ready posts awaiting human approval.

## Objectives
1. Manage multiple social-media accounts through explicit account profiles and permissions.
2. Discover timely and controversial subjects relevant to each persona.
3. Research competing claims, context, original sources, counterevidence and uncertainty.
4. Generate engaging content through clarity, relevance, narrative, useful disagreement and strong hooks.
5. Prepare channel-specific drafts, assets briefs, source notes, proposed publish times and review queues.
6. Keep a human in control of every external submission by default.
7. Learn from post-performance data without allowing metrics to override truthfulness, consent, safety or persona integrity.

## Non-goals
- No fabricated facts, fake citations, impersonation, synthetic grassroots activity, engagement pods, bot comments, mass unsolicited DMs, fake testimonials or undisclosed deceptive sponsorship.
- No deliberate outrage maximization, harassment, doxxing, threats, dehumanization, or targeting people because of sensitive traits.
- No unverified accusations against identifiable people or organizations.
- No automated posting or account actions merely because a date/time has arrived.
- No collection or storage of account credentials in project documents.

## Engagement policy
“Controversial” means a subject with a real, documented disagreement, material public interest, or competing interpretations. It does not mean inventing conflict or escalating hostility. Prioritize substantive engagement: saves, thoughtful comments, shares with context, qualified clicks, audience retention and constructive discussion. Treat raw reach and comment volume as incomplete signals.

## Source and claim rules
- Distinguish fact, interpretation, prediction, opinion, satire and open question.
- Prefer primary sources for the underlying event or claim; add high-quality independent reporting or domain expertise for context.
- Record publication date, event date, author/organization, URL, relevant passage/data, access date and source type.
- Seek counterevidence and the strongest fair opposing argument.
- Never imply a source supports a statement it does not support.
- Mark unresolved or time-sensitive claims for reviewer attention; omit claims that cannot be substantiated.
- Do not turn a claim about a group into a claim about every member of that group.

## Human authority
The human reviewer has final authority over topic, framing, wording, visuals, account, audience, timing and whether to submit. Any substantive edit after approval returns the post to review. Emergency stop and account disablement must be available before any future live integration.

## Canonical lifecycle
DISCOVERED → TRIAGED → RESEARCHED → CLAIM-CHECKED → DRAFTED → EDITORIAL-REVIEW → APPROVED → SCHEDULE-PREPARED → HUMAN-SUBMITTED → PUBLISHED-VERIFIED → ANALYZED.
Alternative terminal states: REJECTED, EXPIRED, NEEDS-RESEARCH, WITHDRAWN, FAILED, UNKNOWN. No state may be inferred from file existence alone.
