## Content-adaptive specialist workspaces: provisional roles, NOT approved formal names
1. Film / episodic fiction: script, treatments, shot planning, production management, continuity, NLE/edit/finish delivery.
2. Documentary / journalism: research, interviews/transcripts, archival provenance, factual verification and accessible versions.
3. Advertising / branded video: campaign brief, client sign-off, format variations, rights/revisions.
4. Music video: synchronized visual planning, live performance/takes, timing, clip-specific visual direction; independent sound/music provider owns stems/mastering.
5. Social/short-form: platform/aspect-format variants, captions, cutdowns, hook/retention tests.
6. Animation/2D/3D/stop motion: storyboards, rigs/assets, DÜRER/Blender scenes, previs/animatics, render layers.
7. Motion graphics and VFX: trackable elements, composites, mattes/passes, shot revisions.
8. Live performance/events/multicam: camera feeds, synchronized timecode, recording, live/near-live production.
9. Educational/technical/YouTube/tutorial: screen capture, narration, demonstrations, subtitles, verification.
10. Hybrid production: overlapping specialist workspaces on the same production identity, assets and provenance.
