Installation & connection

Install & connect

A few simple steps to make THEMBIcouncil feel at home on your devices.

InstallationUsageTroubleshootingApple services

1. Add THEMBIcouncil to your Home Screen

  1. Open THEMBIcouncil in Safari on your iPhone or iPad, or your preferred browser on a computer.
  2. On iPhone or iPad, choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On a desktop, bookmark the website for quick access.
  3. Open the Council and explore its seven personalities and Terry. Use the usage guide whenever you need a hand.

2. Connect your personal Council

THEMBIcouncil keeps your personal connections separate from anyone else's. A private ChatGPT Council invitation and optional cloud connection are issued individually. Open them only in the account they belong to.

Open ChatGPT · Connection help

The website and its Apple-friendly tools work independently of a private Council connection. Private cloud documents and remote device actions require your own authorized access.

3. Add the devices you want to use

iPhone & iPad

Use Safari, the Home Screen shortcut and the Files picker to work with selected iCloud documents.

Windows & Mac

When you have a personal Council device connection, pair your computer using the provided one-time code. You choose which folders and actions to allow.

Android

Install the Phone-MCP companion if you want an independent Android device connection. Its own pairing and permissions are required.

Stay in control

Your files and devices remain yours. No device or account is connected without its separate authorization.

4. iCloud setup

  1. Sign in to iCloud.com directly, using Apple's own sign-in page.
  2. Use Apple services to open Drive, Photos, Notes, Calendar and other supported destinations.
  3. For a selected document, use the local file picker in Safari. On iOS, the Files picker may surface iCloud Drive if enabled in Settings.
  4. Choose which documents to work with; your Apple Account remains under Apple's sign-in and permissions.
