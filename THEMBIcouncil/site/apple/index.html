More Apple destinations

  • Apple Maps: Open Maps; mapping/navigation links are functional without unrestricted location access.
  • Find My: Open Find My in Apple's own session; no remote tracking or device-control grant is implied.
  • Apple TV: Open Apple TV; no universal private playback-library connector is configured.
  • FaceTime / Messages: use device-native user-initiated calls and messaging; the public Council page sends nothing.
  • HomeKit / Home: native HomeKit entitlements and owner-granted accessory permissions are required for real control.
  • Health / Fitness: native HealthKit permission is required for personal health records; no automatic access through a website.
  • Passwords / iCloud Keychain: kept inside Apple system controls. Never export keychains or hand credentials to cloud MCP.
  • iCloud Backup: manage through device Settings. CloudKit and a website do not provide browsing of personal device backups.

Actual AppleRAVEN functionality available for reuse

The live AppleRAVEN Mac provider catalog contains established capability groups for iCloud-aware Files, Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Mail, Messages, Photos, Music, Safari and Shortcuts. It also exposes device and permission status checks.

Those tools run under the currently authorized Mac user's operating-system permissions. They cannot be treated as access to Thembi's Apple Account, Windows laptop, iPhone or iPad. Integration into her private Council requires an independently verified endpoint and account-specific grants.

Integration boundaries

Available now in the deployed site: official Apple links, user-initiated Files/iCloud Drive text-document selection, downloadable local calendar/contact files, Maps/Mail handoffs and source-backed instructions. Independent browser-device acceptance is pending.

Requires additional implementation: Sign in with Apple identity, CloudKit container sync, MusicKit user data, EventKit, Photos/Share extensions, App Intents, HealthKit, HomeKit and any remote device executor.

AppleRAVEN's owner-only Mac credentials cannot be transplanted into THEMBIcouncil; reuse its provider contract and onboarding approach with Thembi's own account and grants.
