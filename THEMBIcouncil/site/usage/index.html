CHORUS & Terry

Choose Heartbi, Fitbi, Dreambi, Bossbi, Roambi, Storybi or SexyBi. All seven are part of one personal Council. Terry is your own separate feline companion and Chief Inspector.

Examples: Heartbi, help me organize this week · Bossbi, organize these job application deadlines · Storybi, suggest a book without spoilers · Ask Terry to inspect the garden gate.

Keep your notes and documents together

Use a personally connected Council to organise text documents, retrieve saved notes and keep track of changes. Your chosen cloud storage and device connections remain separate, so you always know where your files live.

The Council uses account-scoped MCP cloud documents and approved local device paths; this website does not read your files.

Work across your devices

Once your own computer or phone has been paired and authorized, your Council can help with approved file and device tasks. The device asks for permission before carrying out sensitive actions.

Advanced: Council device tools

council.devices.list selects an authorized device. council.device.files.list and council.device.files.read queue approved file operations; council.jobs.result retrieves the result. Terminal actions also require local approval.

Files and documents — through your connector

File operations belong to THEMBIcouncil's authenticated cloud MCP, not to a public browser preview. Open your individually authorized Council connection to access them.

Private Council cloud files

council.cloud.list, council.cloud.read, council.cloud.write and council.cloud.delete act on that Council principal's own cloud documents.

Paired computer files

council.devices.list selects your paired device; council.device.files.list, council.device.files.read and council.device.files.write queue local file actions for approval by the device owner. Verify completion using council.jobs.result.

Connect the MCP · Install and pair a device

On macOS, a user-approved iCloud Drive folder can be included among the connector's permitted local directories after iCloud has synchronized it. This is not unrestricted iCloud account access. Mobile-specific import requires a separate native connector.
