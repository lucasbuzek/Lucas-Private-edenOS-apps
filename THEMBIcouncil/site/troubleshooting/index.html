Connection issues

I cannot connect my personal Council

Make sure you're using the account that received your invitation. Your private Council connection is personal. If your app doesn't offer the required connection option, ask the person who issued the invitation for a compatible setup. Keep invitation URLs private.

My invitation no longer works

Your personal invitation may have expired or been replaced. Request a fresh invitation and reconnect from your own account.

I see the wrong account

Sign out and confirm which account you're using. Personal, family and business accounts stay separate. Do not reuse someone else's private connection or device permissions.

Device issues

No paired devices appear

Open the companion on the device you want to connect and use its current one-time pairing code. Pairing requires that device owner's permission. Then refresh your device list.

Device is offline or command remains queued

Check that the companion is running, your device is online and any requested permission has been approved. If a task is still queued, it has not finished; check again from the Council's activity view.

Windows or Android controls not available

Device actions depend on the companion you've paired. For Android, use the Phone-MCP companion and its separate permission setup. For computers, check your personal pairing instructions.

Apple services and visuals

iCloud files don't appear automatically

THEMBIcouncil does not acquire iCloud files merely because an Apple Account exists. On a Mac, pair the authorized device connector and explicitly allow the local iCloud Drive folder if synchronized; otherwise use an authorized provider connector. The public website cannot read the library.

Photos, Notes, Contacts or Calendar are not syncing

Apple's own apps manage iCloud content. THEMBIcouncil can access only specifically authorized providers or user-selected files. The Council MCP connector does not automatically connect an Apple Account.

Illustrations fail to load

Check your network connection and reload the page. Council illustrations are served from the dedicated Cloudflare image service; access to your Dropbox is not required.

Safe recovery

Never post authentication tokens, private MCP URLs, pairing secrets, iCloud credentials, screenshots of personal data or private file contents. Report only the status, tool name, timestamp, device platform and redacted error.
