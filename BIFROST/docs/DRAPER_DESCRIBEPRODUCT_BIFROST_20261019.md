# BIFROST — CONNECT · KEEP · RESOLVE

**Your private gateway to edenOS.**

BIFROST is being developed as the central interface for connecting your applications, devices, workflows and AI capabilities.

Its aim is simple: give one instruction, recover the relevant context, identify the right tools, coordinate authorized work and report what actually happened.

Designed around local-first operation, user-owned data, independently functioning applications and flexible AI-provider choices.

**Current status:** Private development and testing. Native macOS and local-service candidates have passed automated engineering tests. ChatGPT orchestration, cross-device integration and additional platform experiences remain subject to separate verification.
