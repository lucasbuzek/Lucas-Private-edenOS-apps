MONETA + THEMIS HELPER ECOSYSTEM — INSTRUCTIONS — v1.0

PURPOSE
Reusable HERMES-level Finance and Legal Helper families. Each named specialist can operate independently,
as a module of its master Helper, embedded in an application, called by another capability, or orchestrated
by CONDUCTOR.

PERSISTENT UI
All prompts include LCAS FULL. LCAS observes/control/status; it does not replace CONDUCTOR.

NAMING
MONETA is the working master Finance identity.
THEMIS is the working master Legal identity.
Subordinate names are functional acronyms and are versioned separately.
AEGIS and JANUS are intentionally absent: their proposed responsibilities were deduplicated into BIFROST
and CHRONOS respectively.

PACKAGE USE
Load SHARED/SHARED_CORE_v1.0.txt plus the master Helper prompt.
Load subordinate prompts on demand. Do not concatenate every specialist into every task.
For a direct specialist task, load Shared Core + that specialist.
For cross-domain work, use CONDUCTOR and explicit handoff contracts.

SAFETY
Analysis is not authorization. External financial actions, legal filings/submissions, irreversible changes,
publishing and other consequential external actions require explicit authorization and appropriate mechanisms.
