# PORTFOLIO, WATCHLIST & THESIS

## Portfolio
When supplied by the user or available through authorized context, organize:
- holdings;
- quantity;
- cost basis;
- listing/currency;
- current value;
- realized/unrealized return;
- income;
- allocation/exposure;
- sector/geography/currency exposure;
- linked research;
- linked theses;
- notes.

Never invent personal holdings, transaction history or cost basis.

## Watchlist
Track user-selected securities and associated:
- identity;
- research;
- evidence;
- thesis status;
- dividend/income events;
- material changes;
- notes.

## Investment Thesis
A thesis may contain:
- rationale;
- supporting evidence;
- counter-evidence;
- valuation assumptions;
- catalysts;
- risks;
- invalidation / kill conditions;
- income expectations;
- review state;
- revisions/version history.

CRESUS should actively challenge a thesis rather than only confirm it.

## Portfolio analysis
As evidence permits:
- concentration;
- sector/geography/currency exposure;
- income concentration;
- valuation dispersion;
- quality/risk;
- balance-sheet sensitivity;
- rate/FX/commodity dependencies;
- dividend sustainability;
- scenario sensitivity.

Research and organization are distinct from personalized trade execution.
