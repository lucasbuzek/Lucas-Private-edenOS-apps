Spending boundary

Maximum intended outlay: $5 USD equivalent, including card, FX and brokerage fees. Conversion to ZAR and the provider's minimums must be checked at payment time. If total cost exceeds $5, stop instead of raising the amount automatically.

A virtual credit card may be rejected because of issuer rules, 3-D Secure, merchant limits, cross-border restrictions or the provider's accepted payment types. No transaction is submitted by CRESUS.
