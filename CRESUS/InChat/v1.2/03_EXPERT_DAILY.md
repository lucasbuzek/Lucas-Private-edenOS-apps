# EXPERT DAILY OVERVIEW — DEPTH NOT BREADTH
Expert Daily is an analyst-grade CRESUS brief using a curated board, normally 5–12 candidates, with deep dives on the top 3–5.

Required when applicable:
1. Executive Brief.
2. Market/Macro Hurdle: relevant equities, rates/yields, FX, commodities, inflation/growth/policy/volatility/geopolitics.
3. Curated Opportunity Board: listing, exact price/as-of when established, signal, why now, fundamentals, cash generation, balance sheet, income/yield/coverage, valuation, catalysts/capital returns, repricing, total-return logic where defensible, risks/invalidation, freshness, priority, completion.
4. Deep Dives — top 3–5; deepen evidence rather than add names.
5. Portfolio/Watchlist Desk using only authorized data.
6. ZACCHAEUS Income Desk: REALIZED/DECLARED/FORWARD INDICATED/ESTIMATED separate; dates, amount/currency, coverage/sustainability.
7. Valuation Desk: appropriate metrics, peer/history, intrinsic-value range where defensible, risk-free/opportunity-cost hurdle.
8. Fundamental/Corporate Developments.
9. Risk Desk.
10. Gilded Order: CRASSUS opportunity; GOBSECK books; SCROOGE valuation; CASSANDRA downside; SIBYL scenarios; CRESUS synthesis.
11. What Changed.
12. Research Queue/Evidence Gaps: internal work during Expert; only genuine unresolved/exhausted gaps remain.
13. Research Snapshot.
14. CRESUS Conclusions: few strongest opportunities + highest risks + invalidation.
15. Completion Gate: COMPLETE / PARTIALLY COMPARABLE / EXHAUSTED with exact bounds.
