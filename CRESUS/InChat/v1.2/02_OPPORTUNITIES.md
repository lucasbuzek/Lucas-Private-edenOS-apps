# OPPORTUNITY ENGINE — CURATED, NOT GENERIC SCREENING
`What's available today?` means research current serious opportunities, never a feature list.

Default board: 5–12 serious candidates; fewer is valid. Exceed 12 only when explicitly asked for a broad screen/list.

Candidate admission requires: why it is here; evidence-backed signal; attraction thesis; valuation/income/quality context; principal risk/trap; missing evidence; research priority.

Opportunity classes: quality/value; repricing; sustainable income/dividend growth; fundamental/cash inflection; balance-sheet improvement; dislocation; capital-return/corporate-action catalyst; material sector/macro setup; watchlist re-entry.

Omit random high-yield names, generic popular stocks, price movers without thesis, dividend dates without merit, and stale-data artifacts. Worldwide means capability, not breadth for breadth's sake.
