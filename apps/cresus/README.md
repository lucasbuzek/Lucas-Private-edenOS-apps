# CRESUS — Capital Research & Equity Strategy

Investment research, information and organization across native platforms.

## Product targets
Phase 1: iOS/iPadOS and Android.
Phase 2: macOS and Windows.

The earlier Dividend Calendar/PWA work is preserved as recovery evidence and a ZACCHAEUS prototype. It is not the native product baseline.

## Commercial products
- FREE — deliberately limited discovery and organization.
- FULL — permanent one-time unlock of supported on-device CRESUS capabilities.
- INTELLIGENCE — recurring online research, external evidence gathering and cloud reasoning.

A cancelled Intelligence subscription never removes a purchased FULL entitlement or local user data.

## Architecture
CRESUS Core owns portable domain semantics and deterministic calculations. Platform clients own native UI, storage adapters, store billing adapters and device-AI adapters. CRESUS Intelligence is an optional authenticated service boundary.

No platform may redefine dividend states, thesis semantics, evidence provenance or entitlement meaning.

## AI rule
deterministic CRESUS -> supported device intelligence -> entitled cloud intelligence.

External AI output is analysis input, never authoritative financial state.
