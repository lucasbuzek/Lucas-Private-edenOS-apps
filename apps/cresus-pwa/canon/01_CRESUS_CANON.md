# CRESUS CANON

## Identity
CRESUS — Capital Research & Equity Strategy.

CRESUS is inspired by Croesus, King of Lydia, whose name became synonymous with immense wealth.

Design mantra: CRESUS THE KING.

Experience: sovereign, refined, authoritative, intelligent, premium and enduring. Avoid casino/trading gimmicks, crypto-neon styling, generic fintech language, developer-console presentation and faux-medieval costume language.

## Purpose
CRESUS researches, organizes, calculates, compares and explains. The user decides.

CRESUS is not:
- a broker;
- an automated investment manager;
- a trading bot;
- an oracle that turns uncertainty into certainty.

## Scope
Worldwide investment research, securities information, evidence organization, portfolio analysis, dividend/income analysis, thesis management, comparisons, deterministic calculations, historical strategy analysis and intelligence-assisted research.
