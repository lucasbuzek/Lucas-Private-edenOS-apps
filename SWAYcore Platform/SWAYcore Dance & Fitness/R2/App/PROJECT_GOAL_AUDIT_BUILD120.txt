SWAYcore Build 120 — Project Goal vs Current Build Audit
Date: 2026-09-28

PROJECT GOAL
Functional Alpha with full production UI/UX ready for Xcode/iPhone testing. Core experience must be graphical, music-driven and movement-led; Home/Moves/Beats/Profile are primary navigation while Create, Progress, Avatar, Tracking and Settings remain discoverable. One shared articulated movement truth drives instructor, COACH and user-avatar presentation. Camera tracking is optional but first-class. Music sources and analysis are independent capabilities. Direct .swaypack sharing works without an account/server. Production UI must not be a developer-control surface.

BUILD 119 EVIDENCE REVIEW
PASS/PRESERVE: four-tab shell; secondary routes; local audio import and PCM analysis; generated SWAY Beats; MusicKit/Shazam provider code; Vision camera service and calibration code; workout setup choice; .swaypack validation/import preview/share; persisted profile/avatar/COACH preferences; DesignReferences excluded from runtime.
PARTIAL/UNVERIFIED: production character visual quality; movement teaching quality; workout composition; camera route on device; Apple Music entitlement/service configuration; Shazam device behavior; avatar breadth/visual quality; accessibility/device layouts.
OMISSIONS/ERRORS FOUND: CFBundleVersion remained 118 while CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION was 119; README/pipeline reports were stale; Progress Day Streak incorrectly displayed sessionsCompleted; Create Move could create a new move ID unsupported by the canonical pose engine, producing a non-moving neutral pose rather than a functional authored/remixed movement.
