## 3. 0.2 functional scope and acceptance — every presented control must work
### User journeys and presentation
- **Launch/onboard:** purposeful privacy disclosures; optional camera/microphone/Health/Music/Photos permissions requested only in relevant flows; denial/restriction/settings recovery; off-camera mode always usable. Clear portrait/landscape iPhone/iPad navigation and saved preferences.
- **HOME:** start/resume workouts and quick dance, discover moves, relevant saved progress, actionable shortcuts. No developer labs visible by default.
- **MOVES:** all recovered **26 movement IDs** (March; Step Touch; Hip Sway; Heel Dig; Knee Lift; Double Step; Grapevine; Arm Reach; V-Step; Side Tap; Box Step; Mambo Step; Cha-Cha Basic; Squat Pulse; Side Lunge; Cross Step; Body Roll; Dance Jack; Skater; Pivot Turn; Kick-Ball-Change; Salsa Basic; Jazz Square; Power Knee; Lateral Hop; Turning Grapevine), with real animated 1–8-count Learn/Preview/Practice and mirroring. Existing 21 baked native 3D clips are NOT proof of 26 available moves; ZERO must truthfully animate what is supported.
- **LEARN:** count selection, next/previous, playback/pause, tips, mirror and 20–180 BPM tempo; consistent poses/cues as Workout. Mastery Discovered > Learning > Familiar > Mastered comes from real practice/progress.
- **WORKOUT:** Quick Dance, Dance, Learn Moves, Party, Challenge, Freestyle, Daily Dance, Endurance, Low Impact, Adaptive Workout. Approximately 5–45-minute plans where appropriate, intensity/complexity/style/space/accessibility settings; supported automatic/by-song/continuous/by-routine/manual/repeat advancement; large COACH, count/time/next, Resume/Restart Movement/End; accurate Results, no timer-only false scoring.
- **BEATS/MUSIC:** SWAY-generated offline percussion styles Simple, Pop, House, Hip-Hop, Disco, Latin, Afro, Percussion, Rock, Minimal; compatible local/imported user audio; supported MusicKit/Apple Music behind permission/provider support. One beat/phrase/count clock; BPM/onset/grid confidence and manual fallback, provider-switch and pause/seek/restart consistency; Shazam may preflight unresolved tracks but is not the clock. Never embed protected audio in .swaypack.
- **CAMERA/YOU:** optional on-device Vision plus visible, meaningful privacy control, coherent preview and orientation/mirror mapping, truthful match/not-matched/unknown and loss/reacquisition. EXACT calibration sequence: STEP INTO VIEW > WAVE LEFT > WAVE RIGHT > LIFT LEFT FOOT > LIFT RIGHT FOOT > JUMP > READY TO SWAY. No scored/tracked routine start until READY. Follow-Along bypasses camera/calibration legitimately. No recording/upload of tracking frames.
- **CREATE:** author/edit counts/poses and sequences; Move > Variation > Combination > Routine > Workout/Challenge/Program compatibility; save in local library, reopen/rehearse, validated versioned .swaypack export and import. Files/Share Sheet/iMessage/AirDrop when supported, exact content and metadata round-trip, corrupt/unauthorized reject, nondestructive import.
- **PROFILE/RESULTS:** persistent user choices and progress/learning/mastery, consent-aware HealthKit when enabled, relevant privacy controls, camera and accessibility preferences. Preserve legacy avatar customization UX source and local data; do not claim unfinished renderer selection is functional.
- **RECORD:** explicit Record Me / Record SWAY and user-chosen Photos/Files/share action where permissions permit, with deny/failure handling; no implicit upload. Release/0.2 claims depend on actual recording flow tests, not entitlements alone.
- **ACCESSIBILITY:** VoiceOver, readable typography and counts, Dynamic Type/large controls, Reduce Motion, high contrast, safe layouts, screen-reader order, camera-off operation, low-impact variations and truthful disabled labels.
- **DISCOVERABILITY:** source-backed `Coming to SWAY` for official community, catalog/packs, challenges, additional instructors and Apple Watch; show status without simulated backend results or clickable fake payments.
