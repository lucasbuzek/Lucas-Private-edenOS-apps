## 1. Shared SWAYcore Engine — source of truth
- Movement hierarchy: Move -> Variation -> Combination -> Routine -> Workout/Session -> Challenge -> Program. Move mastery: Discovered -> Learning -> Familiar -> Mastered. Movement complexity is independent of intensity and accessibility.
- Canonical movement definition contains count, pose/angles, side/handedness, mirrored direction, timing, holds, repetitions, transitions, reference pose, workout/learning metadata, version and provenance. The same definition must be rendered in Learn, rehearsal, Workout, scoring, Creator and content exports; never make independent choreography per screen, coach or device.
- Unified temporal contract: available audio rhythm and its measured beat grid/phase -> bar/downbeat/phrase -> displayed count -> move/transition -> COACH animation -> spoken and visual cue -> expected YOU pose -> movement comparison -> score/progression. Half-time/multiple BPM interpretation must preserve beat phase. Shazam is identification/alignment assistance, not the clock.
- Character contract: compatible SWAYHumanoid-v1 23-joint skeleton, art/skin geometry, pivot/weight/deformation and eight heading presentation. Distinguish INSTRUCTOR graphical personality, COACH performance target, YOUR DANCER persistent personal avatar, and YOU optional graphical live tracked representation. Do not substitute one role for another.
- Tracking: optional on-device Vision adapter, truthful confidence and MATCH / NOT MATCHED / UNKNOWN, stale/lost/out-of-frame handling and reacquisition. No camera grants false match/score. Camera-off Follow-Along remains fully usable.
- Accessible timing/cue/progression primitives and offline movement packs. Support domain adapters for Yoga (alignment/holds/breath), Tai Chi (flow/balance/cadence), martial arts (technique/stance/trajectory), dance, and conventional fitness (reps/sets/form), without falsely claiming those products shipped.
- Local-first persistence, compatible app/provider adapters, schema validation, import/export, migrations, malformed/unlicensed content rejection; portable versioned .swaypack is the common interchange, not a parallel community-only movement schema.

## 2. Dance & Fitness — intended complete consumer experience
**Platform and navigation:** native iOS + iPadOS; iPhone 13-class minimum direction, iPhone 15 Pro Max current physical test, iPad portrait and landscape. HOME · MOVES · BEATS · PROFILE primary tabs, with Setup, Calibration, Learn, Workout, Results, Create, Avatar Creator and Settings as flows. Final visual reference: dark/near-black, violet/magenta/hot-pink/cyan, generous character stage, readable hierarchy and accessible touch targets. Do not ship developer/rigging/camera labs in consumer navigation.
**Core loop:** WATCH -> COPY -> MATCH -> COMBO -> SCORE -> UNLOCK -> DANCE. The app is an integrated exercise/teaching system, NOT a camera demo, metronome, generic music player, static dancer collection or studio admin dashboard.
**Workout experiences:** Quick Dance, Dance, Learn Moves, Party, Challenge, Freestyle, Daily Dance, Endurance, Low Impact, Adaptive Workout. Session planning roughly 5–45 minutes as appropriate, configured by time, intensity, complexity, beat/energy, style, small space, accessibility. Advancement supports Automatic / By Song / Continuous Mix and crossfade / By Routine / Manual / Repeat when verified. Pause must genuinely freeze authoritative state; Resume, Restart Move, End are clear. Stop/complete records only earned work and mastery.
**Movement catalog:** approximately 26 bundled core moves in recovered R2, not a 20-move ceiling. Listed: March, Step Touch, Hip Sway, Heel Dig, Knee Lift, Double Step, Grapevine, Arm Reach, V-Step, Side Tap, Box Step, Mambo Step, Cha-Cha Basic, Squat Pulse, Side Lunge, Cross Step, Body Roll, Dance Jack, Skater, Pivot Turn, Kick-Ball-Change, Salsa Basic, Jazz Square, Power Knee, Lateral Hop, Turning Grapevine. Each advertised move needs executable articulated poses, clear 1–8-count flow, camera/reference compatibility, and correct rhythm; user-created/remixed moves retain underlying executable ID and survive export/reimport.
**Learning:** visually inspect counts 1–8 and coherent continuous movement, step-by-step hints and mirroring, Play/Pause/Resume, user-set tempo (recovered 20–180 BPM), same movement/count musical reference in Learn and workout. Learning is available without tracking; camera matching adds truthful feedback only when references exist.
**Beats/Music:** offline SWAY Beats voices (kick, snare/clap, hi-hat/percussion; established styles Simple, Pop, House, Hip-Hop, Disco, Latin, Afro, Percussion, Rock, Minimal); compatible user local/imported audio; authorized MusicKit/Apple Music; playlists, In Order/Shuffle/Automatic; Shazam identification/preflight for unresolved song identity; credible BPM/onset/beat grid/phrase analysis and low-confidence fallback. Protected audio is referenced, NOT copied into public packs. Providers cannot disable local/offline beats.
**Calibration/tracking:** EXACT order STEP INTO VIEW -> WAVE LEFT -> WAVE RIGHT -> LIFT LEFT FOOT -> LIFT RIGHT FOOT -> JUMP -> READY TO SWAY. No workout clock, scoring, choreography advancement or coaching talk before READY. Then countdown/start. Camera-off Follow-Along bypasses this calibration legitimately; Coach continues during loss and YOU fades/reacquires. One stage, not split consumer window or raw Vision skeleton by default. Expected pose, matching and feedback are tied to the musical count and same normalized orientation/mirror transform.
**Guidance:** coach personality/cue semantic agreement across speech and text, non-overlap, VOICE Off/Minimal/Normal/Detailed as established, no false timer-only praise or unsupported tracking certainty. Fitness intensity adapts without changing what was actually performed.
**Create:** author actual counts/poses/moves, preview, save in local library, compose variations/combinations/routines, practice/work out, share portable packages. Professional timeline-heavy creation belongs SWAYZ.
**Record:** Record Me and Record SWAY only when user explicitly activates capture and permits it; save/copy through Photos/Files/native Share Sheet. Camera frames used for tracking are not silently stored or sent. Microphone/Photos errors require graceful recovery.
**Progress:** true learning mastery, program/challenge history, sessions, achievements/unlocks, HealthKit permission-safe workout records when enabled; Apple Watch companion/control concept near v1 rather than proven in Alpha. Local persistence and privacy; profile saves survive relaunch/import where specified.
**Accessibility:** camera opt-out, Low Impact/Adaptive choices, reduce-motion, voiceover, large controls, high contrast, readable counts, route discoverability, meaningful disabled states, portrait/landscape safe layout.
**UI functional contract:** EVERY shown tab, card, arrow, button, selection, gesture, app menu, camera choice and setting must do what it claims. Final UI evidence is actual device-visible screens; compile and numerical pose tests alone do not establish product UX.
