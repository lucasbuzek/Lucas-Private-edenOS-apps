**Recovery:** 2026-10-10. **Evidence status:** family product/business architecture RECOVERED; economic terms UNKNOWN / NOT APPROVED. This document is deliberately self-contained and must travel with SWAYcore full backups.

## 1. Binding business/product relationships
SWAYcore is ONE CORE. MULTIPLE EXPERIENCES. The source of truth is one underlying movement/choreography/pose/timing/content engine, consumed by distinct products, not five competing movement systems.
- **SWAYcore Dance & Fitness:** iOS/iPadOS flagship consumer experience, dance-learning, workouts, rhythm/music, original customizable YOUR DANCER, COACH and optional tracked YOU, personal Create and local portable choreography.
- **SWAYZ:** a professional instructor and choreography-authoring tool, CREATE · CHOREOGRAPH · TEACH · INSPIRE, with movement counts/poses/transitions, music-sync timelines, rehearsal, portable packs, student instruction. Not a studio billing system.
- **SWAYcore Kid Dancers:** child-first instruction, FUN · CONFIDENCE · CREATIVITY · FOUNDATIONS; parent/safeguarding boundary, no automatic adult community, direct messages or public-child profiles.
- **SWAYcore Studio Management:** an independent professional business operations tool, INSTRUCT · MANAGE · GROW · SUCCEED; schedules, recurring classes, staff/instructors, student members, rosters, attendance, memberships, invoices/payment status and basic finance. Studio billing its members is NOT SWAYcore itself charging the studio.
- **SWAYcore Engine/Core:** reusable foundation for Dance, Yoga, Tai Chi, martial arts, fitness and future domains. These extensions are not automatically released commercial products.
- **SWAY future dance specialist:** distinct future dedicated learning concept; not automatically live, synonymous with SWAYZ, or substituted for Dance & Fitness.
Alpha/current technical direction is offline/local-first for all suitable core features. Official community is a separate OPTIONAL online adapter required for future OFFICIAL social/catalog/publishing scope, not a mandatory account for local dancing or local exports. Current family products beyond Dance & Fitness are recovered specifications/source, not verified shipping apps.

## 2. Who receives value and from which product (RECOVERED FUNCTIONAL SCOPE)
**Personal learner / dancer:** learns real count-by-count moves, practices adjustable tempo, dances workouts to generated/compatible music, optionally uses on-device camera-based comparison, customizes an avatar, creates/shares a move and tracks learning/activity. No login or network may become a prerequisite for the established local functions.
**Individual dance teacher / choreographer:** uses SWAYZ to author/arrange counts, movement poses, timing, music, cues and routines once; exports/interchanges with consumer Learn/Workout in the same canonical definition. Creator identity, author attribution and licensing must remain attached.
**Studio manager/operator:** runs legitimate private studio class/roster/staff/student/membership/payment administration. Content references may link to SWAYZ and participant teaching, but member records and payment details must never leak into public choreography/community data.
**Parent/guardian / child learner:** accesses age-appropriate safe learning, with camera optional and on-device by default. Exact ages, guardian accounts, child progress sharing and purchase consent requirements are NOT YET specified.
**Official community participant / original author:** opts into pseudonymous social identity if wanted; discovers approved packs; favorites/follows, challenges, original uploads and downloads; fully controlled privacy, provenance, moderation, report/block and deletion. Local sharing continues independently.
**Future movement instructors / specialists:** may reuse Engine for other movement disciplines under additional product-specific requirements, rights and physical validation.
