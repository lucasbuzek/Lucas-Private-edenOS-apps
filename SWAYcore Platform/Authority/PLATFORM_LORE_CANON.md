## Purpose / platform story
SWAYcore turns music, expressive movement and learning into accessible, shareable experiences. A movement can be understood, practised, taught, choreographed, assessed, and reused by distinct products without changing its fundamental timing or meaning. WATCH → COPY → MATCH → COMBO → SCORE → UNLOCK → DANCE is the established consumer learning loop, not a compulsory UI or business workflow for every product. The future platform should keep movement and creativity available offline and private by default.
